Privacy Policy for Post Prachaar

Last Updated: October 2026

Welcome to Post Prachaar. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy notice or our practices with regard to your personal information, please contact us.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you register on the app, express an interest in obtaining information about us or our products and services, or otherwise when you contact us.

The personal information that we collect depends on the context of your interactions with us and the app, the choices you make, and the products and features you use. The personal information we collect may include the following:

  • Personal Details: Name, Phone Number, Email Address.
  • Business Details: Business Name, Business Address, and Business Logo/Profile Picture (used solely for generating your posters).
  • Device Data: We may collect device IDs for authentication and security purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information collected via our app for a variety of business purposes described below. We process your personal information for these purposes in reliance on our legitimate business interests, in order to enter into or perform a contract with you, with your consent, and/or for compliance with our legal obligations.

  • To facilitate account creation and logon process: We use your phone number (via OTP) or email to set up your account.
  • To generate customized content: The personal and business details you provide are dynamically placed onto templates to generate custom posters for you.
  • To fulfill and manage your orders/subscriptions: We may use your information to fulfill and manage your premium subscriptions made through the app (e.g., via Cashfree).

3. Will Your Information Be Shared With Anyone?

We only share information with your consent, to comply with laws, to provide you with services, to protect your rights, or to fulfill business obligations. We may share your data with third-party vendors, service providers, contractors, or agents who perform services for us or on our behalf and require access to such information to do that work (e.g., Firebase for authentication and database hosting, Cashfree for payment processing).

4. How Long Do We Keep Your Information?

We keep your information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this privacy notice unless otherwise required by law. You can request the deletion of your account and personal data at any time by contacting us.

5. How Do We Keep Your Information Safe?

We have implemented appropriate technical and organizational security measures designed to protect the security of any personal information we process (such as utilizing secure Firebase infrastructure). However, despite our safeguards and efforts to secure your information, no electronic transmission over the Internet or information storage technology can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions or comments about this notice, you may email us at our designated support email address.
